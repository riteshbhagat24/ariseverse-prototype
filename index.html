    The Real Estate Market Health Diagnostic

    Five metrics to tell whether a market is fundamentals-led or speculation-led — before it corrects. Score any market with public data from DLD, RERA, portals and developer disclosures.

    The law of motion

    If price growth outpaces income growth plus infrastructure growth, the system becomes speculative. If speculation exceeds end-user absorption, volatility becomes inevitable.

    Case reference

    Why Dubai 2008 ≠ Dubai today.

    Dubai's post-2009 recovery came from outgrowing speculation with real economic expansion — not from speculation resolving itself.

    2008

    High leverage, heavy investor-to-investor flipping, off-plan units traded multiple times before completion. Prices fell ~50% in some segments.

    Today

    Materially higher cash-transaction share, a broader end-user and long-term investor base, and continued infrastructure and population inflow underpinning demand.
